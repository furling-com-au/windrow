Field review 27 Aug 2026 Windrow — EP grain supply chain simulation rev c08513c

Windrow Field Review

The evidence spine of this project is genuinely strong, the engine does almost everything it claims, and the honesty discipline in the docs is better than most published models manage. Almost none of that survives the trip to the screen. The default view tells first-time visitors the model is wrong by 1771%. The flagship rail scenario promises 2.5 million truck-km saved and then reports freight going up. The season labelled "live" has no weather, no ships and no crop data, and the copy calls it the season as it really ran. Every finding below was reproduced — in the running app, by instrumenting the engine, or by redoing the arithmetic.

Verdict

The model is better than the app says, and the app says things the model doesn't support

Those are two different problems and they compound. The physics is calibrated to ~1% on season totals; the presentation layer then manufactures errors that aren't there, and asserts conclusions the engine contradicts.

Season-total accuracy −1.0% 2025/26 finishes 2.227 Mt simulated vs 2.249 Mt observed. Across three seasons −0.4% to −1.6%, including the held-out one. This is the real headline and the app never leads with it.
What the default screen says 1771% "Reality check", 3 Nov, default season, nothing touched: "1771% ahead of reality." The same unchanged model reads +252% one day and +82% the next.
Rail scenario freight +$197k "Bring back the trains" promises ~2.5M truck-km saved. The only trucking figure shown says freight got worse. The 2.74M truck-km the model does save is computed and thrown away.
Clicks to a hard crash 2 Bumper harvest + more grain to Lucky Bay, default season, throws QUEUE INSANE at Thevenard: 408 and dead-ends the app with a raw exception string.
Touch targets at 44px 0 / 24 On a 393px viewport, with 56% of the panel hidden behind an unindicated inner scroll and type down to 7px — for a phone-first rural audience.
Fitted params pinned at a bound 5 / 12 rateScale, matShift, retentionShare, portAttractBias and luckyBayBias all sit exactly on their lower search bound. The report presents all twelve as an interior fit.

Theme 1 · Credibility

The app is its own worst reviewer

The single highest-damage defect in the project, and among the cheapest to fix. It is the first number a new visitor reads.

F1Critical Measured liveArithmetic

"Reality check" compares a continuous curve against a week-stale step, and reports the gap as model error

app/src/components/LeversPanel.svelte:181–192 (simVsActual), rendered :456–461 · app/src/App.svelte:238–249 (KPI "actual")

Both loops keep the last weekly report whose week_ending <= sim.day, then divide by it. The simulated cumulative advances every tick; the observed cumulative only exists on Sundays. During peak harvest that denominator can be six days and ~490,000 t old. 

Default view, 2025/26, "The season as it happened", untouched:

   3 Nov 2025   "0.16 million t ... vs 0.01 reported  —  1771% ahead of reality"
   6 Dec 2025   "1.44 million t ... vs 0.78 reported  —    86% ahead of reality"
  ~day 140      "2.23 million t ... vs 2.25 reported  —     1% behind reality"

Arithmetic at 6 Dec:
  observed cum, week ending 30 Nov     776,461 t   <- what the app divides by
  observed cum, week ending  7 Dec   1,222,446 t
  implied rate                           63,712 t/day
  interpolated to 6 Dec              1,158,734 t

  sim 1.44 Mt vs 776,461     ->  +85%  (displayed)
  sim 1.44 Mt vs 1,158,734   ->  +24%  (like-for-like)

So roughly two-thirds of the headline is manufactured by the comparison — at day 62 the split measures as ~29 points real, ~21 points artefact. Because the denominator jumps weekly, the same unchanged model reads +252% one day and +82% the next.

Fix

Compare only at week-ending dates and label it "as at week ending 30 Nov". Suppress the percentage while the denominator is small — it is meaningless in October either way. Then lead with the metric that is actually good: season totals inside 1.6% on three seasons.

F3MajorSourceMislabel

Everything labelled "vs the real season" is measured against a second simulation run

app/src/components/LeversPanel.svelte (ROW_TIPS, sentences, "Delivery pace") vs app/src/worker/sim.worker.ts:83–113 (computeBaseline, defaultParams())

The baseline lifecycle is otherwise sound — always defaultParams() for the loaded season, nulled on season change, season-checked on receipt, cached with correct invalidation. The problem is purely the words: "~7 days behind the real season", "30% longer than the real season", "vs the real season at the same date". Say "the modelled normal season". The project draws that line rigorously everywhere else, which is why this reads as a slip rather than a stance.

F4CriticalHeadless, reproduced

"Bring back the trains" reports freight going up, because trainset tonne-km land in the truck accumulator and are priced at the road rate

packages/sim/src/engine.ts:842 (trains share tonneKm) · priced at LeversPanel.svelte:208–209, rendered :127–133

engine.ts:842   this.tonneKm += (minutes / 60) * 75 * load;
                 // in stepLinehaul — taken by trainsets too; isTrain (:800)

rail patch (railReinstated + linehaulTrucks 32), 2025/26:
  tonneKm    302,307,208 -> 304,279,214    +1.97M (+0.65%)
  renders    "+2.0M t.km  ·  +$197k freight"   in the amber "worse" colour

  truckKm     17,636,975 ->  14,896,096    -2.74M truck-km saved
  kpi.truckKm exists (engine.ts:1038) — grep app/src: zero uses

The story card promises "about 2.5 million truck-km" saved, the lever tooltip repeats it, and A21 documents it. The 2.74M saving the model actually produces matches that promise — and is never shown. Two related defects compound it: trainsets run on the road travel-time matrix at road speeds, reaching 4.0 cycles/trainset/day (12,800 t/day) against the "~2 cycles/day ceiling" asserted in A21, docs/scenarios.md and the engine's own comment; and the "road line-haul fleet cut by one-third" in A21 is applied only by scripts/scenarios.ts, not by the app's rail lever.

Then either give trains a rail transit time or enforce the stated cycle cap, and correct the "~2 cycles/day" claim in all three places.

Theme 3 · Arithmetic

Numbers in the takeaways panel that are simply wrong

Not framing issues — computation defects, each reproduced against the real engine.

F9CriticalHeadless, reproduced

"Delivery pace" prints hundreds of days of nonsense — the crossing-day search has no bound and no fallback

app/src/components/LeversPanel.svelte:221–226, rendered :141–148 and :270–276

while (j < b.receivedByDay.length && (b.receivedByDay[j] ?? 0) < s.kpi.receivedT) j++;
paceLagDays = s.day - j;      // no bound, no fallback

(a) run exceeds the baseline's season total -> j runs to 365:
    rail   day 140 -> -225   day 200 -> -165   day 300 -> -65
(b) baseline stops receiving at day 96, so j freezes for ~270 days:
    fleet 500  day 364 -> +273      drought  day 364 -> +299

Rendered: "~299 days behind the real season" / "~275 days ahead".

Worst case: at day 200 the rail scenario clears no other threshold, so its
ONE bullet under "Barely any difference from the real season." is
  "Deliveries run about 165 days ahead of the real season."
Fix

Return null when the search hits the end of the array or the baseline series has plateaued, and render that as "not comparable" rather than a day count.

F10MajorHeadlessUndocumented constant

An undocumented flat 75 km/h converts travel minutes into every kilometre and dollar the model reports

packages/sim/src/engine.ts:747, :842, :1038 · vs data/ASSUMPTIONS.md A5

engine.ts:747   const km = (tr.legMin / 60) * 75;   // "mean 75 km/h (A5-derived)"
engine.ts:842   this.tonneKm += (minutes / 60) * 75 * load;
engine.ts:1038  truckKm: Math.round((this.truckTravelMin / 60) * 75)

The matrix carries ONLY minutes, built at A5's class speeds:
  trunk/primary 90 · secondary 80 · tertiary 70 · unclassified 60 · residential 40
75 contradicts every one of them, and grep '75 km' over data/ docs/ README: nothing.

Consequence, worked: Wudinna -> Port Lincoln is 143.3 matrix minutes,
  reported as 179 km where the 90 km/h network implies ~215 km.
So trunk line-haul is understated ~17% and low-class farm legs overstated up to 25%
  (of 17.6M truck-km: 13.3M farm->site, 4.4M line-haul — net sign indeterminate).
What IS understated ~17% is the rail saving, which is all trunk line-haul —
  including the "2.5 million truck-km" a reader sees in the scenario story.
Fix

Export leg distances from the OSM routing step and accumulate real km. If that's deferred, register the 75 km/h constant as its own assumption and state which displayed figures lean which way.

F11MajorHeadless

The travel-time assumption slider fabricates $7.9m of freight although no truck drives an extra kilometre

packages/sim/src/engine.ts:747–748, :842 (time-derived km) · slider at LeversPanel.svelte:406–409

"tonne-km" is really tonne-HOURS x 75, and legMin is already scaled by travelTimeScale.

travel times x1.30  ->  "+79.2M t.km  ·  +$7.9m freight"   deliveries move -1,992 t
travel times x0.80  ->  "-51.8M t.km  ·  -$5.2m freight"        deliveries move   +209 t

Simple mode says: "Trucks travel further overall — grain gets double-handled

This is the panel whose own instruction is "if a conclusion flips when you nudge one, treat it with care" — and the conclusion it manufactures comes from a pure speed assumption.

F15CriticalMeasured liveHeadless

After any scrub, the narrator asserts "peak harvest. Grain is pouring in" for the rest of the year

app/src/App.svelte:43–49, 66–71, 78 (resetHistories + backfill) · app/src/lib/narrator.ts:27, 36, 50–52

On a jump, resetHistories() empties dailyReceived and the backfill loop starts at
snap.day exactly — so every lower index is a HOLE. Then:

Theme 6 · Assumptions vs code

The register has drifted from the model it documents

This is the part of the project that most deserves to be trustworthy, because the whole design rests on it. Three of the register's stated sensitivities are contradicted by what the shipped model measurably does.

data/ASSUMPTIONS.md:72–74, :78 · packages/sim/src/engine.ts:275–277, gates at :598, :615 · SiteDetail.svelte:11, :38 · app/public/data/sites.geojson
